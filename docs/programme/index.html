Preliminary Programme

Preliminary schedule for CHR2027

The programme below is preliminary and subject to change. Session titles, presentation details, rooms, and social activities will be confirmed when the final programme is published in December 2026.

Lunches and mid-morning and mid-afternoon refreshments are included in the relevant registration fees.

Tuesday, 5 January 2027: Workshops

Four half-day pre-conference workshops will take place, with two running in parallel during the morning and two during the afternoon. Each workshop is priced and registered for separately from the main conference. Lunch is included for all workshop participants.
