Campus Accessibility

Accessibility information for CHR2027 at The University of Manchester

We want CHR2027 to be welcoming and accessible to everyone attending the conference. Detailed information about the accessibility of the conference buildings and rooms will be added once the programme and room allocation have been confirmed.

Access requirements and additional support

We are happy to provide additional services and support where possible. If you have any access requirements or need specific arrangements in order to participate in the conference, please let the local organisers know as far in advance as possible by emailing local@computational-humanities-research.org. This will give us the best opportunity to discuss your needs and make suitable arrangements.

Building and room accessibility

The University of Manchester provides information about accessible buildings, rooms, routes, parking, and related support:

  • Building and room accessibility guidance
  • Detailed University of Manchester access guides on AccessAble
  • University campus interactive map

The AccessAble guides provide detailed information about access to individual University buildings and facilities. The University guidance also identifies resources for accessible routes and teaching spaces. Please check this page again nearer the conference for information about the specific CHR2027 venues.
